# Caldrek

> [!note]
> **Scaffold example.** Demonstrates the shape of a Settlement concept.

## First impression

> [!read-aloud]
> Caldrek is a city that has agreed not to look down. Its streets are laid over older
> streets, its docks bolted to drowned docks, and at low tide you can see the windows of
> the city underneath, still shuttered.

## Three things a visitor notices

1. Every building has two front doors — one at street level, one bricked up a storey below.
2. Nobody swims. Not for sport, not for work. The harbour divers are all foreigners.
3. Funerals are held at the waterline, and the Choir sings at all of them.

## Powers that be

| Who | Holds | Wants |
|-----|-------|-------|
| The Harbourmaster's Office | Docking rights, the drowning rolls | Surfacings declared a hazard, so salvage law applies |
| [The Drowned Choir](/factions/the-drowned-choir.md) | Funeral rites, public sympathy | The schedule unnoticed |
| [Serath Vane](/npcs/serath-vane.md) | The contract trade | The party paid and pointed elsewhere |

## Sites worth a scene

- **The flooded archive** — holds the city's drowning rolls, going back past the
  surfacings. Waist-deep, lit by whatever you bring, and the harbourmaster's office would
  rather nobody went down there.
- **The waterline steps** — where the Choir sings. Public, always watched.
- **The low market** — trades in surfaced goods, asks no questions, knows the schedule
  without knowing it knows.

> [!secret]
> The archive is the cleanest route into
> [Who wears Serath's face?](/threads/who-wears-seraths-face.md) — the rolls list Serath
> among the drowned. The harbourmaster's reluctance is unrelated and entirely financial,
> which is what makes it good cover.

## If the party leaves

Caldrek keeps running the clock in
[the Drowned Choir](/factions/the-drowned-choir.md). Coming back to a changed city is
better than a city that waited.
