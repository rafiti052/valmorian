# The World

## Themes

What the campaign is *about*, underneath the plot. Keep this to three entries. They are
the tiebreaker when you are improvising and two options seem equally good — pick the one
that presses a theme.

1. **Inherited memory.** What you remember is not the same as what happened to you.
2. **Cities built on cities.** Nothing is new; everything is standing on something drowned.
3. **Patience as menace.** The antagonists are not in a hurry, and that is the frightening part.

## Facts of the setting

Only what changes play. A calendar detail that never reaches the table is a hobby, not
worldbuilding.

- Relic contact grants borrowed memories. The memories are accurate and are not the
  toucher's. There is no known way to remove them.
- Divination consistently fails around surfaced relics — it returns *someone else's*
  answer.
- The drowned empire has no surviving name. Everyone calls it "the drowned one," and
  nobody finds that strange, which is itself a clue.
