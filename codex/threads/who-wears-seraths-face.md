# Who wears Serath's face?

> [!note]
> **Scaffold example.** Demonstrates the shape of a Plot Thread concept.

**Pressure:** `urgent` · **Arc:** [The Drowned Crown](/arcs/the-drowned-crown.md)

## The question

The party trusts [Serath Vane](/npcs/serath-vane.md). Serath Vane drowned eleven years
ago. Who has been handing them contracts?

## Who wants what

| Party | Wants the answer to be |
|-------|------------------------|
| [The Drowned Choir](/factions/the-drowned-choir.md) | Never found. Serath is their best asset. |
| Caldrek's harbourmaster | Found loudly, so the harbour contracts are voided. |

## The clock

Four ticks. Advance one per session in which the party does not investigate.

- [x] **Tick 1** — Serath refers to an event he could not have attended.
- [ ] **Tick 2** — A dockhand who knew the real Serath goes missing.
- [ ] **Tick 3** — Serath asks the party to retrieve the eighth relic personally.
- [ ] **Tick 4** — The ninth surfacing begins. Serath stops pretending.

## Ways in

The party can crack this from several directions. Do not gate it behind one.

- **Records.** Caldrek's drowning rolls list Serath. They are in a flooded archive.
- **People.** Anyone who knew him before the drowning notices he is left-handed now.
- **Magic.** Any effect that detects undeath, illusion, or possession fails — which is
  itself the clue, because it should have detected *something*.

## If resolved

Set `pressure: resolved`, add the resolving session to `sources`, and open the successor
thread: *what does the Choir do with an asset it can no longer hide?*
