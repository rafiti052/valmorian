# House Rules

Baseline is **D&D 5e, 2024 rules**. Anything here overrides the books.

*No house rules recorded yet.*

## Why record rulings

Mid-session you will make a call to keep things moving. The problem is not the call — it
is making the *opposite* call four sessions later. Log it here the moment it happens; one
line is enough.

Recommended frontmatter: `type: House Rule`, `visibility: public` (players should be able
to read every one of these), and `sources` linking the session where it was first ruled.

Template:

```markdown
---
type: House Rule
title: Short name
description: One sentence stating the rule.
visibility: public
status: stable
sources:
  - resource: /sessions/session-04.md
    title: First ruled at the table
---

**RAW:** what the 2024 books say.
**Here:** what we do instead.
**Because:** the reason. Future-you will want this.
```
