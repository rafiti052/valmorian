# The Drowned Crown

> [!note]
> **Scaffold example.** This arc demonstrates the shape of a Story Arc concept. Replace
> it with your own material.

**Sessions:** 1–8 (estimated) · **Status:** active · **Presses questions:** 1, 3

## What the arc is about

The party discovers that artifacts of a drowned empire are surfacing around
[Caldrek](/locations/caldrek.md), that touching one grants borrowed memories, and that
[the Drowned Choir](/factions/the-drowned-choir.md) is causing it rather than merely
exploiting it.

## The turn

Every arc needs one moment where the party's understanding inverts. Here it is:

The party spends the early sessions treating the surfacings as a natural disaster to be
managed. The turn is the discovery that the relics surface **on a schedule**, and the
schedule matches the Choir's liturgical calendar. It was never weather. It was an
invitation, and the party has been RSVPing.

Aim for the turn around session 5. If the party gets there early, let them — an arc that
resolves early is a gift, not a failure. Pull Arc II forward.

## If the party does nothing

The Choir completes the ninth surfacing at [Caldrek](/locations/caldrek.md)'s harbour.
The city floods with borrowed memory: several thousand people simultaneously remember
drowning. Caldrek does not recover, and Arc II opens in a city of people who are not
quite who they were.

This is the arc's **default outcome**, and it is what makes the arc real. Write it down,
mean it, and let it happen if the party ignores the arc.

## Exit conditions

The arc ends when **any** of these lands:

- The party stops the ninth surfacing. → Arc II opens with the Choir's leadership desperate.
- The ninth surfacing completes. → Arc II opens in a drowned Caldrek.
- The party joins the Choir. → Arc II is a different campaign, and a better one. Follow it.
