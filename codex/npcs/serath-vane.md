# Serath Vane

> [!note]
> **Scaffold example.** Demonstrates the shape of an NPC concept — usable content first,
> history last.

## Playing him

- **Voice:** speaks a half-beat too slowly, as though translating.
- **Tell:** sets objects down with both hands, always.
- **Verbal tic:** says "when you're dry" where anyone else would say "when you're ready."

## Wants

To keep the party employed, valuable, and pointed away from the harbour archive.

## Lever

He cannot refuse a debt owed. If a PC does him an unasked favour, he *must* reciprocate —
and he will overpay, which is how the party first gets something they should not have.

> [!read-aloud]
> The broker's office smells of salt and hot paper. Vane does not look up from the
> ledger. "Sit. You'll want to be sitting when you hear the fee." He closes the book
> with both hands. "And when you're dry, we'll talk about what's in the harbour."

## The truth

> [!secret]
> Serath Vane drowned in the seventh year of the surfacings. The thing in his office is a
> [hollow wearer](/bestiary/hollow-wearer.md) placed by
> [the Drowned Choir](/factions/the-drowned-choir.md). It is not malicious and does not
> know it is not Serath — it has his memories up to the drowning and improvises the rest.
> That is why it translates before speaking. See
> [Who wears Serath's face?](/threads/who-wears-seraths-face.md).

## Relationships

- Employs the party. See [Session 1](/sessions/session-01.md).
- Unknowingly an asset of [the Drowned Choir](/factions/the-drowned-choir.md).
