# The Drowned Choir

> [!note]
> **Scaffold example.** Demonstrates the shape of a Faction concept.

## What the party thinks

A drowning-cult. Harmless, tolerated, sings badly at funerals.

## What is true

> [!secret]
> The Choir is running the surfacings on a liturgical schedule. They are not summoning
> the empire back — they believe it never left, and that the living are the ghosts.
> [Serath Vane](/npcs/serath-vane.md) is theirs.

## Wants

1. Complete the ninth surfacing at [Caldrek](/locations/caldrek.md).
2. Keep the schedule from being recognised as a schedule.
3. Recruit, gently, anyone who has touched a relic and kept the memories.

## Will not do

Killing is loud and the Choir is patient. They will discredit, relocate, and drown-by-
accident long before they will assassinate. Knowing a faction's *limits* is what lets you
improvise them consistently.

## If the party attacks them

The Choir does not fight back in kind. They go public as victims, and Caldrek's
authorities side with them. The party's reputation takes the damage instead.
