## What happened

- The party was in [Caldrek](/locations/caldrek.md)'s low market when the seventh relic
  surfaced in the harbour. Public, chaotic, three people drowned in the crowd surge.
- [Serath Vane](/npcs/serath-vane.md) found them within the hour — faster than he should
  have — and offered a retrieval contract for the *next* one.
- The party accepted. Nobody asked how he knew there would be a next one.
