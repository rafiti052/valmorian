# Session 2 — plan

> [!note]
> **Scaffold example.** Demonstrates the shape of a Session Plan. Plans go stale — note
> the `stale_after` field.

## The one thing

If only one thing happens tonight: **the party retrieves the eighth relic and one of them
touches it bare-handed.** Everything else is optional.

## Opening

Pick up in [Serath Vane](/npcs/serath-vane.md)'s office, mid-negotiation. Start *after*
the boring part of the conversation.

## Beats

| Beat | Purpose | Cut if short on time |
|------|---------|---------------------|
| The dive briefing | Establish that locals won't dive — and that Vane knew the depth already | No |
| The retrieval | The relic; the memory-flood on contact | No |
| The flooded archive | Optional. Only if a PC asks about the drowning rolls | Yes |
| Choir at the waterline | Seed tick 2 — the missing dockhand | Yes, but seed it in a rumour instead |

## Personal beat owed

The PC who noticed nobody swims gets the answer, from a drunk at the low market: *"You
don't swim where you might be recognised."*

## Prepared, not planned

- **Stat block:** [hollow wearer](/bestiary/hollow-wearer.md), in case the party gets
  aggressive with Vane early. They might. Let them.
- **Name list:** Teska, Orlun, Marovic, Bright Anne — for anyone who needs a name fast.

## If the party goes sideways

They will. The two things that survive any derail:

1. Vane knows things he should not.
2. The Choir's schedule is a schedule.

Deliver those two facts by *any* route the session offers.
