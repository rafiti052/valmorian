## Naming

- Plans: `session-NN-plan.md`, `type: Session Plan`
- Recaps: `session-NN.md`, `type: Session Recap`

A plan is not edited into a recap. Keep both — the gap between what you planned and what
happened is the single most useful diagnostic you have about your own GMing.
