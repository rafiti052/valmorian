Narrative planning work: **$ARGUMENTS**

Delegate to the **plot-weaver** subagent.

Route by intent:

- **"I'm lost" / "where is this going"** → it runs the recovery procedure from
  `codex/arcs/index.md` and reports *the situation* — what is pressing, who is owed a
  beat, what the factions did — plus a few directions with their costs. Not a plan.
- **A thread name** → advance or resolve it. On resolution, keep the file, mark
  `resolved`, and propose the successor pressure.
- **"new"** → open a thread: the question, who wants which answer, the clock in ticks,
  honest `pressure`.
- **Arc-level work** → what it is about, the turn, exit conditions, and the default
  outcome if the party does nothing.

Remind the GM of the house rule that matters most here: the campaign's shape is his call.
The weaver presents options with costs; it does not decide what the campaign is about.
